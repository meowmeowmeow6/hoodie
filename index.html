Jordy,

Four years. I've had four years of walking into a room and looking for you first, and it still hasn't worn off. I don't think it's going to.

I'm not good at saying this part out loud, which is probably why I made you a whole hoodie instead. But the thing stitched on the pocket is a real recording of me, and the shape of it is the shape of my voice saying something I mean. You are wearing it now. That's the closest I could get to handing it to you.

Thank you for being patient with the projects, the late nights, the parts on the kitchen table. Thank you for laughing at the right things. Thank you for four years of being the best part of my day, reliably, without ever seeming to try.

Happy birthday. Turn the page — there's more.

— C
